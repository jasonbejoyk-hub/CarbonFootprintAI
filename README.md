

# CarbonFootprintAI

# Overview

Obtains historical Indian Carbon Emissions dataset from 1990-2014.

The data is cleaned and analyzed. After the analysis, regression is used to find the relation between the year and carbon footprint emissions. 

A policy slider is also included to help simulate different policy scenarios and estimate the future emissions. 

Lastly, the results are visualized to better understand the relationship between policies and projected emissions.
